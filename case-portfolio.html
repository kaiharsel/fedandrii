FED.ANDRII

You are already on it. This page and every other one I made myself, from design to launch, in four languages

Languages English, Ukrainian, Polish, German
Scope Design, site, animations
Site fedandrii.me

The task

A portfolio has to be proof of what it claims. Showing other people work and nothing of my own is a weak position, so the first work here is the site itself

The second condition was clients in Europe. That means more than one language, and not a switch over a single text but a separate page for each

The solution

Everything is made from scratch: the design, the layout, the animations, the texts. Nothing was taken ready-made

Four languages - English, Ukrainian, Polish and German - each at its own address. A visitor language is detected automatically, but a link that was sent always beats the guess: a link to the Polish version opens Polish

The animations are written by hand: the heading letters fall and gather back, the footer burns with fire made of characters, and on a wide screen the portfolio scrolls sideways together with the page

The result

You are reading this case on the very site it is about

A link can go to a client in Poland or Germany and they read it in their own language, not in English out of necessity
