About the project

Task

The client needed a site where a visitor can not only read about the service but pay for it on the spot, with no back-and-forth and no invoices issued by hand

Solution

Built the site in Framer around one continuous path: service page, plan choice, payment in a few steps. The payment provider is wired in directly and confirmation is sent automatically

Project screens

Home page The service and its value
Plan page What is included and the price
Payment form Step by step, no spare fields
Confirmation Order status after payment
